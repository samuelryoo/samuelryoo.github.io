About

I like the layer where software meets hardware.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering student at UC Irvine. I am happiest when a system misbehaves and I have to find out why: a noisy supply, a floating pin, two devices that disagree about a serial port.

The signals I work with keep getting smaller: logic levels, then sensor data, and now the electrical activity of muscles and the brain. A brain-computer interface needs what a robot needs, low-noise electronics and real-time firmware, applied to much fainter signals. That is where I am heading. See current research and the EMG hand.

Off the bench

Away from the bench I play acoustic guitar and golf, which I played on varsity in high school. I follow the Dodgers, cook ad hoc, and go backpacking in the San Gabriel Mountains. In 2026 I spent a summer living and working in Tokyo.
